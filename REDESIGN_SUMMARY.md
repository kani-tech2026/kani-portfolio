## 🖼️ Add Your Profile Photo

**Your hero section is ready for your professional photo!**

1. Save your photo as `profile.jpg` to the `public/` folder
2. Run: `python save_image.py "your-photo-path.jpg"`
   OR
3. Manually copy photo to: `c:\Users\Admin\Desktop\portfolio\public\profile.jpg`

The photo will appear in the hero section with:
- Beautiful cyan glow border
- Glassmorphism frame effect
- Professional accent lines
- Auto fallback to "K" avatar if image missing

**Photo Specs:**
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Dimensions: Portrait (320×420px optimal)
- Quality: High-res professional photo
